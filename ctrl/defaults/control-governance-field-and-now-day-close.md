# The day closes

**Status:** distributed default — becomes human source on adoption

A moving horizon that never closes becomes a junk drawer. What makes NOW useful is that it ends: what was live is carried, what finished is removed, and what the day held stays readable in a dated record that later edits cannot rewrite.

DAY is a closure reading at a local civil date I supply or you read from the field — never a timezone guess, never a scheduler's opinion. The close runs in one order, and the order is the law:

```text
read the field            human scratch, agent returns, open promotions
classify                  carry what is live; release what resolved, expired or promoted
snapshot first            the day keeps byte-exact copies of what it closed over
derive the reading        the dated record is written from the snapshot, before anything is cleaned
then clean                carried records stay at their stable paths and gain the day in their lineage;
                          released records leave the moving field unless a preserve ref protects them
```

My scratch is mine: the close copies it into the day's sources and leaves the live copy under my hand. Your returns are attributed and bounded; the close moves them to their day's keeping or releases them, never quietly deletes evidence.

Human words stay in the day in my own words where they render. A day that cannot close honestly says so and stops before it cleans — a partial close that names its failure beats a clean claim that lied.

At the project register the close is `projectcentral.now.rollover`; at the root register the same law runs through the session-strap procedure until Central ships the native root action. Same policy file, same semantics, both fields.

The point of the boundary is tomorrow: the field opens clean, the questions that mattered are still visible, and yesterday remains a reading, not a rummage.
